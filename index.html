Clinical Experience • EDM 595 A / B / C

Library Media Internship Hub

A total of 100 clinical hours bridging practical learning with AASL National Standards. Experience can be completed across school, public, and university library settings.

EDM 595 A Prereq: EDM 581

Professional Responsibilities

MUST be completed in a P-12 School Setting

  • Knowledge of learners and learning
  • Instructional partnership & advocacy
  • Collection development & funding
  • Ethics and professional standards
EDM 595 B Prereq: EDM 580

Access to Information

School, Public, or Academic Library

  • Information technology integration
  • Ethical information-seeking behavior
  • Networking with library community
  • Effective instructional delivery
EDM 595 C Prereq: EDM 583

Impact on Student Achievement

School, Public, or Academic Library

  • Reading promotion & literacy strategies
  • Strategic planning & assessment
  • Leadership & professional development
  • Community advocacy
ALSDE Reciprocity & FAQ • South Alabama Media Program

Frequently Asked Questions & Contact Directory

Answers to certification reciprocity, course transfers, MLIS evaluations, and degree program admissions.

You may be eligible for Alabama certification through certificate reciprocity. ALSDE requires:

  • Valid out-of-state professional educator certificate in Library Media.
  • At least a master's degree.
  • Two full years of qualifying full-time professional educational experience in a P-12 school system.

Possibly. Certificate reciprocity generally does NOT apply without an educator certificate. You would complete an Alabama-approved Library Media certification program. Up to 9 credit hours of prior graduate work may transfer.
