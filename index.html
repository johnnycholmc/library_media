Library Media Certification FAQ

Quick answers regarding reciprocity, certification pathways, and graduate credit transfers[cite: 3, 4, 5].

I hold an out-of-state Library Media certificate. Can I transfer it to Alabama?
You may be eligible through certificate reciprocity if you hold a valid out-of-state professional certificate, at least a master's degree, and 2 full years of P-12 educational experience. The Alabama State Department of Education (ALSDE) makes the final determination[cite: 3].
I have an MLIS but no Library Media certificate. Can I become a school librarian in Alabama?
You can explore our Alabama-approved Library Media certification program. Previous graduate coursework is reviewed, and up to 9 credit hours of eligible coursework may apply toward program electives[cite: 4, 5].
I already have an Alabama teaching certificate and master's degree. Do I need another full master's?
Not necessarily. You may be eligible for a certification-only pathway focusing primarily on Educational Media core courses and internships, subject to official transcript review[cite: 5].
Can my previous MLIS or graduate courses transfer?
Up to 9 credit hours of graduate-level coursework may potentially transfer toward elective requirements after syllabus and transcript review[cite: 5].
What is the easiest way to determine my pathway?
If you have an out-of-state certificate, check ALSDE reciprocity[cite: 7]. If you have an MLIS without certification, explore our program[cite: 7]. If you hold an AL certificate + master's, ask about the certification-only option[cite: 7]. Request a transcript review to clarify your best route[cite: 7]!
