ALSDE Reciprocity & FAQ • South Alabama Media Program

Library Media Certification FAQ

Answers to certification reciprocity, course transfers, MLIS evaluations, and degree program admissions.

You may be eligible for Alabama certification through certificate reciprocity.

For Library Media, Alabama generally requires:

  • A valid out-of-state professional educator certificate in Library Media
  • At least a master’s degree
  • Two full years of qualifying full-time professional educational experience in a P–12 school system

ALSDE makes the final determination regarding reciprocity eligibility.

Start here: Official ALSDE Out-of-State Reciprocity Portal

No.

An MLIS and professional library experience are valuable, but Alabama educator certification has separate requirements.

For out-of-state Library Media reciprocity, ALSDE specifically requires qualifying educator certification and professional educational experience.

Experience in an academic, public, or medical library should therefore not automatically be assumed to satisfy the P–12 experience requirement.

If you do not already hold a professional Library Media educator certificate, an Alabama-approved Library Media certification program may be the more appropriate pathway.

Your previous graduate degree and library coursework should be reviewed to determine how much of your prior education can apply toward the program.

The ALSDE reciprocity document requires applicants in instructional support areas such as Library Media to verify two full years of full-time professional educational work experience in a P–12 school system.

If you do not meet that requirement, contact ALSDE to determine your available certification options.

The Alabama State Department of Education (ALSDE) makes the official certification determination.

The University of South Alabama can help you understand your educational program options, but ALSDE determines whether an out-of-state certificate qualifies for Alabama reciprocity.

Your previous degree, transcripts, coursework, and certification status need to be reviewed by the Library Media program and certification office.

Depending on your background, you may qualify for different pathways, and eligible prior coursework may reduce the number of courses you need.

I have an MLIS but no Library Media educator certificate → Explore the USA Library Media certification program
I have an Alabama educator certificate + master’s degree → Ask about the certification-only pathway
I have previous graduate coursework → Request a transcript review for possible transfer/elective credit
