Frequently Asked Questions & Contact Directory

Answers to certification reciprocity, course transfers, MLIS evaluations, and degree program admissions.

You may be eligible for Alabama certification through certificate reciprocity. ALSDE requires:

  • Valid out-of-state professional educator certificate in Library Media.
  • At least a master's degree.
  • Two full years of qualifying full-time professional educational experience in a P-12 school system.
