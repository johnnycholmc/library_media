Eligibility & GPA Criteria
  • Bachelor's degree from accredited institution.
  • 2.5 Minimum GPA on degree transcript or last 60 hrs.
  • Valid Class B or Class A teaching/instructional support certificate.
  • At least 2 years full-time educational work experience.
Application Checklist
  • Online Application ($60 processing fee).
  • Official transcripts from all institutions.
  • Current Resume / Curriculum Vitae.
  • 2-page double-spaced applicant narrative statement.

ALSDE Reciprocity & FAQ • South Alabama Media Program

Frequently Asked Questions & Contact Directory

Answers to certification reciprocity, course transfers, MLIS evaluations, and degree program admissions.

You may be eligible for Alabama certification through certificate reciprocity. ALSDE requires:

  • Valid out-of-state professional educator certificate in Library Media.
  • At least a master's degree.
  • Two full years of qualifying full-time professional educational experience in a P-12 school system.
Official ALSDE Out-of-State Reciprocity Portal

Possibly. Certificate reciprocity generally does NOT apply because it requires holding an active out-of-state professional educator certificate.

Instead, you will complete an Alabama-approved Library Media certification program. At South Alabama, previous graduate coursework can be evaluated for up to 9 credit hours of transfer credit into the elective portion.

Not necessarily.

ALSDE's instructional support reciprocity pathway requires your out-of-state certificate to be in the same instructional support area (Library Media). You will need to pursue the USA Library Media certification program.

Not necessarily.

Depending on your background, you may qualify for a certification-only pathway. Prior coursework can be reviewed for up to 9 credit hours, allowing you to focus primarily on Educational Media core courses and internships.

Yes!

Up to 9 credit hours of graduate-level coursework may potentially be accepted toward elective requirements after review based on course content, graduate credit, and syllabus relevance.

No.

ALSDE specifically requires qualifying educator certification and 2 full years of P-12 educational work experience. Experience in an academic, public, or medical library does not automatically satisfy the P-12 requirement.

Who decides reciprocity? The Alabama State Department of Education (ALSDE) makes the official determination regarding out-of-state certificate transfers.

Who decides course requirements at USA? Dr. Johnny Cho and the CEPS certification office review your transcripts and background.

Can I talk to someone before applying? Absolutely! Prospective students are strongly encouraged to reach out to Dr. Cho at ycho@southalabama.edu or join our community Discord.
